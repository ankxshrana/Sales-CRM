# CRM Foundational Research

Before starting the data modeling phase, I researched core CRM concepts to ensure the architecture aligns with real-world business strategies.

## What is a CRM?
Customer Relationship Management (CRM) is a technology framework and business strategy designed to manage interactions with customers throughout their lifecycle. By centralizing communication, it ensures interactions are personalized, timely, and impactful.

## Key Functions Relevant to this Project
Based on my research, this project falls under the category of an **Operational CRM**, which focuses on streamlining customer-facing processes like sales. The core functions include:

*   **Information Acquisition & Contact Management:** Centralizing customer details (Companies) and organizing data from multiple touchpoints into detailed profiles.
*   **Sales & Lifecycle Management:** Tracking every stage of the customer journey (Deals and Pipeline Stages) to help identify opportunities and forecast revenue.
*   **Analytics & Reporting:** Providing data-driven insights into team performance and customer behavior (Dashboard & Metrics).
*   **Team Collaboration:** Bringing sales reps and managers together under a unified strategy with shared customer records and visibility.

## Why it Matters for Sales Teams
A structured CRM allows sales professionals to manage leads, track communication history, and close deals faster through a single, unified view of the pipeline, preventing data loss and duplicated efforts.
